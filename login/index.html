Member Terms & Conditions

Last updated November 2026 · LUMIST, 2750 Las Amigas Road, Napa, CA 94559

1. Who can join

Membership is open only to people who are of legal drinking age: 21 or older in the United States, and the legal age where you live if you are elsewhere. By creating an account you confirm you meet this requirement. We may ask for proof of age at any time, and an adult signature with valid ID is required on delivery.

2. Your account

You are responsible for the information you give us and for keeping your password private. Please use a password you do not use anywhere else, and tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account. One account per person.

3. The allocation list

Joining the allocation list gives you early notice of new releases. It does not guarantee a purchase, a quantity or a price. Allocations depend on how much wine each vintage produces and may be limited per member.

4. Prices and orders

Prices shown before release are indicative. Final prices, taxes and shipping costs are confirmed before you pay. An order is accepted only when we confirm it by email. We may cancel an order, with a full refund, if a price or listing was shown in error.

5. Shipping and alcohol laws

We can only ship to places where the law allows it, and some U.S. states and countries restrict or ban direct shipping of wine. Alcohol may not be sold or delivered to anyone under the legal drinking age or to anyone who appears intoxicated. Wine is for personal use and not for resale.

6. Your privacy

We collect only what we need to run your account, handle orders and, if you agree, send you news about LUMIST. We do not sell your personal information. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, and you can unsubscribe from emails at any time.

7. Ending membership

You can close your account at any time. We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, gives false information about age or identity, or is used for resale.

8. Changes and governing law

We may update these terms and will tell members about significant changes before they take effect. These terms are governed by the laws of the State of California. Please enjoy our wine responsibly.
