Cover story · Essay

The space between notes.

On going back to the same place, again and again, and finding there is always more to see.

Some kinds of attention only come from returning. The same row in different light, the same hill changed by a season. A place does not get smaller when it becomes familiar; it gets deeper. We think a bottle can be met the same way: with time, curiosity, and no one telling you what you should notice.
